About this directory

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) — AI improving the process of building AI itself — is a new frontier, and it may decide how fast everything else in AI moves. No one has closed the loop yet. This list is a framework for tracking that progress.

Companies are working toward it from different angles, and it is no longer only a frontier-lab game: small teams with tight loops can make AI evolve remarkably fast.
